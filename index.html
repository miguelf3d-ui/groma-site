From brief to a prototype you can click

From diagnostic
to running.

Nothing for you to specify, nothing to migrate, no twelve-month programme. Weeks from the first conversation to a system working inside your business — connected to the software you already pay for, run by the people already there.

01

Diagnostic

Forty-five minutes, free, no deck. We walk the real process — the spreadsheet one person maintains by hand, the inbox that is actually a queue, the report that eats a morning every week. You leave knowing what is worth automating and what is not.

02

Scope

Exact deliverable, exact date, exact price, and the one number we agree to be judged on. If the payback is not there, we say so before you have spent anything.

03

Something you can click

Within days, a working interface in your browser — real states, real data shapes, the awkward cases included. Approving a picture of a system is how projects fall apart in month three.

04

Build and connect

We build it and wire it into what you already run: your ERP, your inbox, your invoicing, your spreadsheets. No migration project, no "first you need to replace X".

05

Hand over

Your team is trained on the thing they will actually use, with documentation written for them rather than for a developer. You own the code, the data and the accounts. Nothing is held hostage.

06

Measure and extend

We track the number from step two. What earns its keep gets extended; what does not gets cut. Most systems justify a second phase — and when one does not, you will hear it from us first.

In practice

An industrial flooring applicator was fielding "how much per square metre?" by phone — a question nobody can answer honestly without seeing the floor. Sales time went into visits that were never going to convert. We replaced the call with a five-question intake that qualifies the enquiry before anyone gets in a van, and a back-office the team edits without calling us. Same trade, same people, one fewer wasted morning per week.
