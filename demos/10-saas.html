The month-end tax

Reconciliation is still a spreadsheet problem at companies doing €200M+

Every subscription business ends up with the same three failure modes. They're not caused by bad accountants — they're caused by systems that were never designed to agree with each other.

~14 hrs / cycle

CSV matching by eye

Payout files from three PSPs, a bank statement in MT940 and an ERP export that uses a different invoice key. Someone sorts by amount and hopes nothing shares a value.

Day 5 crunch

The close compresses into 48 hours

Nothing can be reviewed until the feeds land, so five days of work gets done in two. Controllers approve journals at 21:40 because the board pack ships tomorrow.

PBC list risk

Adjustments nobody can explain

A €40k plug clears the variance in March. In September the auditor asks who approved it and against which document. The email thread is gone and so is the analyst.

How it works

Three surfaces. That's the whole product.

Ingest, decide, close. Ledgerline doesn't try to be your ERP or your billing system — it sits between them and makes sure they agree.

01 — Matching engine

Rules you can read out loud in an audit

Start with exact reference matching, then add tolerance rules for the messy 12%: FX rounding, partial captures, aggregated payouts, customer-side reference typos. Every rule is versioned, and every match records which rule fired.

  • Amount and date tolerances — absolute or percentage, per source and per currency pair.
  • Many-to-one matching — one €48,210 Adyen payout against 84 invoices, netted of scheme fees.
  • Dry runs on history — test a rule against the last 6 closes before you enable it.

02 — Exceptions queue

The break is highlighted before you open the record

Ledgerline doesn't just tell you two records failed to match. It shows both sides field by field, marks exactly what differs, proposes the treatment, and keeps the approval trail attached to the entry forever.

  • Field-level diff across payment, invoice and journal — no tab-switching between four systems.
  • Ownership and SLA — breaks route by entity, size and age; anything over 5 days escalates.
  • Immutable trail — who resolved it, on what evidence, exported straight into your PBC pack.
